Task exposure

Sec. 5.7.3

Exposure of one execution is intensity × time: I = wE·rate/refE + wR·max(0, REBA−1)/(refR−1) + wB·max(0, Borg−2)/(refB−2), x = I·t (exposure-minutes). The Ch. 4 ERI counts each task once per cycle, whatever its duration.

Simulated shift

Sec. 5.7.5 & 5.8 · one example shift (3 blocks of 2 h 30 min)

Main scenario: the true REBA of tasks 20, 38 and 44 is higher than the observed value. No rotation: each operator stays on one loop. Offline rotation: rotation at the breaks with the initial values. Real-time: estimates updated every cycle (after 5 warm-up shifts), rotation with the measured exposure and the Borg rule (Borg ≥ 7 ⇒ not to the heaviest loop). The same operators and noise are used for the three policies of a headcount; this is one shift; the last chart adds the means over 10 seeds.
